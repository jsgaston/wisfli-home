Charlie reads gold on closed bars and counts conditions. When a breakout, reversal, momentum or mean-reversion stack reaches its threshold, it sells once — then manages that single position until the stop, the target, the time limit or Friday closes it.

Closed bars only

While a trade is open the EA takes no new entries on that symbol. There is no grid, no averaging and no lot multiplier.

How Charlie decides

Four independent condition stacks run on every closed bar. Each one counts how many of its conditions are true and fires when it reaches its minimum. They are checked in order, and the first one that qualifies is the one that trades.

S01 — Breakout

Bearish break of structure, a close under the recent support, trend strength on ADX, a volume spike and a strong-bodied bearish candle. Two of those five are enough.

S02 — Reversal

Change of character after higher lows, an upper-wick rejection, an overbought RSI and a stochastic cross down. Two of four.

S03 — Momentum

MACD below its signal, MACD under zero, negative momentum over the lookback, a volume spike and selling pressure in the ticks of the last seconds before the bar closes. Two of five.

S04 — Mean reversion

Price pressed against resistance, an upper-wick rejection, overbought RSI and the upper Bollinger zone. Two of four.

Anything that cannot be computed counts as false

If an indicator buffer is not ready, or there are too few ticks to measure pressure, that condition is simply not active. The EA never guesses a value in order to trade.

Before the order

Spread ceiling, one position per symbol, cooldown in bars since the last entry, maximum trades per day, daily loss stop, optional hour and weekday filters, and a Friday cutoff.

While the trade is open

Stop and target in points or in ATR multiples, break-even once the trade is far enough in front, optional trailing stop, a maximum holding time and a forced close on Friday afternoon.

After every bar

A CSV parity log records the timestamp, the signal, whether the entry was allowed, the reason codes and the full feature snapshot — so any decision can be replayed and checked.

What Charlie is not

It is not a signal service and not a managed account: you run it on your own terminal and you stay in control of the settings. It does not promise a return, and gold on M1 is a fast, spread-sensitive market — broker conditions change the outcome more than they do on slower systems.

The rule set was reconstructed from an analysis of historical XAUUSD behaviour, and the price-action detectors (structure breaks, rejections, tick pressure) are our own definitions. Run it in the Strategy Tester on your own broker's data before putting money behind it.

How the licence works

Charlie is licensed software. After payment you receive the compiled .ex5 by email together with a key, and the EA checks that key when it starts and while it runs.

Your key

The key looks like WSF-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. You paste it into the EA inputs. One key works on up to three MetaTrader accounts, so your demo, your live account and a backup terminal are all covered.

The check is made against api.wisfli.com over HTTPS. The EA sends the key and the account number, nothing else — no trade data, no credentials, no personal information.

Allow the address in MetaTrader

MetaTrader blocks outbound requests unless you list the address. Open Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL and add https://api.wisfli.com. This is the single most common reason a first run fails.

If you cancel

You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. After that the EA stops opening new positions — and only that. Existing positions are never closed for you: stops, targets, break-even, trailing and the time exit all keep working so nothing is left unmanaged.

Managing the subscription

Everything — invoices, card changes, cancellation — is at api.wisfli.com/manage. You cancel yourself, whenever you want, without writing to anyone.

If something goes wrong

Write to support@wisfli.com with the line the EA printed in the Journal. That line usually says exactly what the problem is.

Questions

If yours is not here, write to support@wisfli.com.

Can I backtest it before subscribing?

The Strategy Tester is the right place to judge any system, and we encourage it. Ask at support@wisfli.com what is available for Charlie — when a free backtest build exists it is linked on this page, and it runs in the tester only, not on a live or demo chart.

The EA says it cannot reach the licence server.

Nine times out of ten the address is not allowed in MetaTrader. Add https://api.wisfli.com under Tools → Options → Expert Advisors → Allow WebRequest for listed URL, exactly like that, with no trailing slash. If it still fails, a firewall or the VPS provider may be blocking outbound HTTPS.

Does it need a permanent internet connection?

It needs one to validate the licence periodically. Short outages are tolerated and open positions keep being managed. A VPS is the sensible setup anyway, since a system reading M1 bars should not depend on your laptop staying awake.

What happens to my trades if the subscription ends?

New entries stop. Everything else continues: stop loss, take profit, break-even, trailing and the time exit are all still applied, so no position is abandoned. Nothing is force-closed by the licence.

Can I use it on more than one account?

Yes, up to three MetaTrader accounts with the same key — typically a demo, a live account and a spare terminal. Sharing the file or the key outside your own accounts is not permitted and is the one thing that gets a licence revoked.

Do demo accounts need a licence?

Yes. The subscription covers the software, not the account type, so a demo chart validates the key exactly as a live one does.

Why does it only sell?

The rules were derived from short-side behaviour on gold and no buy-side rule met the same standard, so none was invented to balance the system. Charlie does one thing. If you want the long side too, run it next to another system rather than expecting this one to cover both.

Which broker and which account type?

Any MetaTrader 5 broker offering XAUUSD, hedging or netting. What matters far more than the brand is the spread and the commission on gold during the hours the EA is active: on M1, a wide-spread account will change the result materially.

Can I change the settings?

Every parameter is exposed as an input: thresholds, stops, filters, sizing, session hours. The defaults are what runs on the Armada account, so if you change them the published results no longer describe your setup.
