RegimeSwitch Pro

Expert advisor for MetaTrader 5

A robot that works out what kind of market it is in before it decides whether to trade. It fits a hidden Markov model to the price history in your own terminal, estimates the probability of the next state, and combines that with a volume-confirmed reversal rule.

Every symbol and timeframe has to pass an out-of-sample edge test before it is allowed to place a trade. The ones that fail sit out and say so in the journal.
